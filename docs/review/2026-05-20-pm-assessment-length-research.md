# PM Assessment Length Research

Date: 2026-05-20

## Why The App Used 21 Questions

The 21-question length came from the original MVP spec, which said the full mock should simulate a "boss skill" Senior PM assessment. The topic distribution was:

- Product Analytics: 4
- Data Literacy: 3
- Chart Interpretation: 4
- Inductive Reasoning: 3
- Data Interpretation: 4
- A/B Testing: 3

That totals 21. It was a target-assessment match, not a broad industry standard.

## External Benchmarks

There is no universal product-management assessment standard. Public assessment vendors use different lengths depending on whether the test is a short hiring screen, a multi-measure assessment, or a psychometric role-fit test.

- TestGorilla says its library tests are 10 to 30 minutes, and each multiple-choice test uses 12 to 20 questions from a larger question bank: https://support.testgorilla.com/hc/en-us/articles/9027723634331-Comprehensive-guide-to-tests
- Adaface's Product Manager Online Test is 40 minutes with 15 Product Management MCQs and 5 UI/UX MCQs, for 20 total questions: https://www.adaface.com/assessment-test/product-manager-assessment-test
- iMocha's Product Manager Test lists 20 minutes and 10 questions: https://www.imocha.io/tests/product-manager-assessment-test
- Blackblot's product-management psychometric tests list 80 to 110 questions, but those are personality or role-fit psychometric tests rather than a short analytical PM screening mock: https://www.blackblot.com/bpt

## Recommendation Applied

Use a 20-question full mock.

Why:

- It fits the common short-screening range of 10 to 20 questions.
- It matches the Adaface Product Manager example exactly at 20 questions.
- It keeps the current 30-minute timer clean: 20 questions gives 90 seconds per question.
- It remains broad enough to cover all six topics while keeping the mock focused.

Updated distribution:

- Product Analytics: 4
- A/B Testing: 4
- Data Literacy: 3
- Chart Interpretation: 3
- Inductive Reasoning: 3
- Data Interpretation: 3

Product Analytics and A/B Testing get the extra slots because they are the most product-management-specific analytical areas in this app. The remaining topics still appear in every full mock with three questions each.
